## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Ethos
This design system embodies finance-grade precision, operational discipline, systemic connectivity, and calm clarity. Designed for founders, operational executives, and financial leaders, the aesthetic rejects the neon gradients, playful blobbiness, and micro-animations of consumer software in favor of institutional trust, deliberate hierarchy, and uncompromised visibility. It balances the computational authority of a double-entry ledger with modern, humane clarity.

### Visual Style Movement
The design movement is **Corporate Modern with High-Clarity Technical Restraint**. It avoids hyper-skeuomorphism and decorative glassmorphism. Surfaces are clean, architectural, and grounded by low-contrast borders, solid structural fills, high-contrast ink typography, and intentional dark-navy contrast anchors. Depth is communicated through calibrated surface layers rather than dramatic drop shadows.

### Emotional Response
Interactions evoke control, certainty, stability, and speed. Users experience zero cognitive friction or layout ambiguity; dense operational flows (such as Quote-to-Cash or Procure-to-Pay) feel systematic, traceable, and calm.

## Colors

### Hierarchy & Application Rules
- **Primary Teal (`#0F766E`)**: Reserved strictly for high-priority interactive conversion elements (primary CTAs, key focus active states, validated inputs) and active navigation pills. Used with surgical restraint to maintain its semantic weight.
- **Secondary Navy (`#102A43`)**: Provides executive authority and grounding. Utilized for major anchors, structural navigation bars, technical trust badges, and contrast-inversion sections (e.g., Trust & Infrastructure sections and global footer).
- **Tertiary Mint (`#2DD4BF`)**: Functions strictly as an active flow accelerator—directional node indicators, stage progress highlights in workflow pipes, and small high-visibility telemetry dots. It is never used as standard body or card background fills.
- **Neutral Ink (`#172B4D`)**: The primary typographic voice, delivering optimal contrast against white cards and light canvas backdrops without the harshness of pure black.
- **Canvas (`#F5F7FA`) & Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Establishes crisp spatial containment. Base canvases use muted gray-blue, while transactional workspaces, tables, and metric cards elevate to pure white surfaces bounded by structural borders.
- **Dark Surface (`#0B1724`)**: Deepest contrast base for foundational security, audit log modules, and code-grade ledger views.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Rules
- **Headings (Manrope, 600–800)**: Manrope provides modern geometric precision with stable horizontal proportions. Headlines must use tight letter-spacing to deliver confident editorial and enterprise weight without feeling sterile.
- **Body & Controls (Inter, 400–700)**: Inter ensures legibility across complex multi-column tables, ERP schemas, data inputs, and high-density labels.
- **Financial & Numerical Data**: All monetary quantities, inventory counts, audit dates, and ledger rows must activate font features `tnum` (tabular numerals) and `zero` (slashed or distinct zero) to ensure column alignment in dense operational views.
- **Eyebrows & Metadata**: Uppercase micro-labels in 11px Inter Bold with `letterSpacing: 0.08em` are systematically used to categorize roadmap milestones, workflow states, and subsystem indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Philosophy & Breakpoints
The layout adopts a disciplined **12-column fluid grid** constrained to a `1440px` maximum viewport on desktop to guarantee predictable reading lines and stable dashboard metrics.

- **Desktop (1200px – 1440px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Hero visual splits 5:7 between text framing and live interactive dashboard previews.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px margins. Complex multi-node sequences reflow to 2x2 modular blocks or directional scroll bands.
- **Mobile (375px – 430px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Multi-column cards stack strictly vertically into single-column layouts with edge-to-edge touch targets.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an exact 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem = 8px`, `1rem = 16px`, `1.5rem = 24px`, `2.5rem = 40px`). Form fields and card paddings strictly conform to internal tokens (`space-md` to `space-lg`), eliminating arbitrary gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

### Structural Tonal Stacking
Rather than depending on heavy diffuse shadows, elevation is achieved via **low-contrast architectural outlines and calibrated surface fills**:

1. **Floor / Base Canvas (`#F5F7FA`)**: The foundation level of the operational workspace.
2. **Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary functional containers, data cards, form groups, and dashboard panels sit on white surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px `#D7E0E8` structural border.
3. **Ghost Elevation (Resting Cards)**: Surfaces use an ultra-subtle, ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(16, 42, 69, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(16, 42, 69, 0.02)`.
4. **Interactive Hover / Flyout**: Modals, dropdown pickers, and active workflow cards rise smoothly using:
   `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(16, 42, 69, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(16, 42, 69, 0.04)` with a shifted border tone to `#0F766E` at 20% opacity.
5. **Dark Contrast Anchors (`#102A43` / `#0B1724`)**: Deep structural sections invert the color scheme, using `#172B4D` inner borders to establish depth without diffuse blur.

## Shapes

### Corner Geometry
The system employs **Rounded Level 2**, matching the balanced, purposeful geometry of modern business software:
- Standard UI interactive components (buttons, input fields, badges): `0.5rem` (8px).
- Functional cards, data grids, and modular panels: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Large dashboard viewports and section containers: `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Status indicator tags and micro-pills: Full radius (`9999px`) where appropriate for tags.

### Vector Details
In alignment with the brand monogram, line terminals on technical workflow nodes, timeline connectors, and process schematics use precise, rounded stroke caps (`stroke-linecap="round"`) with an active stroke width calibrated to 2px or 3px relative to component scale.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0F766E` background, `#FFFFFF` Inter SemiBold text, 8px border-radius (`0.5rem`), padding: 12px 20px. Hover: `#0D655E`. Active: `#0B544E`. Focus: 2px offset outline in `#2DD4BF`.
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#D7E0E8`, text `#172B4D`. Hover: `#F5F7FA` surface with border tone `#526579`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle**: Transparent background, text `#102A43`, hover background `#E5EBF0`.

### Workflow Cards & Connectors (The "Connected Movement" Signature)
- Horizontal pipeline cards representing lifecycle stages: **Lead → Quote → Order → Invoice → Payment → Ledger**.
- Cards use `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#D7E0E8` borders, padded with `1.25rem`.
- Active stage lines are linked by 2px `#D7E0E8` connectors with a centered 8px circular node. When completed or active, lines illuminate to solid `#0F766E` with an interior accent pulse of `#2DD4BF`.
- The terminal "Ledger" node features an authoritative navy-tinted tag indicating balanced double-entry validation.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#D7E0E8`, 8px radius, text `#172B4D`, placeholder `#526579`. Height: 44px for accessible desktop/mobile touch targets. Focus state: 1px solid `#0F766E` with a 3px ring in `rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square/circle with `#D7E0E8` borders; selected state fills `#0F766E` with crisp white iconography.

### Chips & Phase Indicators
- **Phase 1 (Live/Core)**: Subtle Teal background (`rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.08)`), text `#0F766E`, border `1px solid rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.2)`.
- **Phase 2 (Roadmap/Operations)**: Subtle Blue/Navy background (`rgba(16, 42, 69, 0.06)`), text `#102A43`, border `1px solid #D7E0E8`.
- **Phase 3 (Expansion)**: Neutral slate background (`#F5F7FA`), text `#526579`, border `1px solid #E5EBF0`.

### Accordions (FAQ)
- Clean, non-bordered horizontal cards separated by 1px `#E5EBF0` dividers.
- Expansion triggers use bold Manrope titles (`18px`) that shift color from `#172B4D` to `#0F766E` when active, with smooth rotational chevron indicators.

### Metric Cards
- White surface, 16px corner radius, containing an upper eyebrow label (11px, `#526579`), a prominent Manrope numeral (28px tabular, `#172B4D`), and a bottom directional micro-badge (`#0F766E` or `#526579`) tracking period deltas.
